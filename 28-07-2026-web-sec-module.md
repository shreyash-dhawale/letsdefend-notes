## Module: Network Log Analysis

### Key concepts
1. Examine firewall, proxy, DNS, and network logs to identify suspicious connections and unusual activity.
2. Detect suspicious IP addresses, domains, ports, protocols, and communication patterns that may indicate an attack.
3. Connect events across multiple log sources to trace attacker behavior and support incident response.

### What anomalous traffic looks like
Pattern 1: **Unusual traffic volume** – A sudden spike or drop in network traffic compared to normal baseline behavior.
Pattern 2: **Unexpected communication** – Connections to unknown or suspicious IP addresses, domains, unusual ports, or at abnormal times, indicating potential malicious activity.
