## Module: SIEM 101

**Key concept 1:** The first step for training the SIEM tool is to collecting log data.

**Key concept 2:** After collecting log data, SIEM tool aggregates that log data which is used for parsing, filtering and enrichment(i.e. increasing the efficiency of collected log).

**Key concept 3:** Performance of SIEM tools is determine by speed which is depend on the storage of logs which helps in the next and last step of SIEM i.e. Alerting. It process after log collected, processed and stored where analyst need to find abnormal behaviours.

## Module: Incident Management 101

**Key concept 1:** Every security incident follows a structured process: Identification, Triage, Investigation, Containment, Eradication, Recovery, and Lessons Learned to ensure threats are handled effectively.

**Key concept 2:** Incidents are classified based on their severity, impact, and urgency so SOC teams can focus on the most critical threats first and allocate resources efficiently.

**Key concept 3:** Throughout the investigation, analysts should document all findings, actions taken, and evidence collected while communicating with the appropriate teams to ensure accurate reporting and future improvements.

## Module: Phishing Email Analysis

**Key concept 1:** Review the email header to identify the real sender, trace the email's path, and verify authentication methods like SPF, DKIM, and DMARC to detect spoofed emails.

**Key concept 2:** Inspect suspicious URLs, attachments, domains, IP addresses, and file hashes. Verify them using threat intelligence tools such as VirusTotal to determine if they are malicious.

**Key concept 3:** Look for social engineering tactics such as urgency, fake login pages, impersonation, and unusual requests. Based on the findings, classify the email as malicious or benign and recommend appropriate response actions, such as blocking the sender, quarantining the email, or reporting the incident.
